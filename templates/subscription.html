Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I upgrade?

When you upgrade to Premium, you'll immediately gain access to all premium features including unlimited participants per room, Slack integration, and automatic scheduling.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your premium subscription at any time. Your premium benefits will continue until the end of your current billing period.

How is payment handled?

All payments are securely processed through Stripe. We never store your credit card information on our servers.
